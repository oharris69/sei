T-contrast

Brand accents fail contrast as text on light, and white fails on them

Cyan #00c0f3 on white = 2.14:1 (hero line 2, 80px) and green #a6ce39 on white = 1.82:1 (“connective tissue”, 48px), both below the 3:1 large-text floor. White on cyan = 2.14:1 for 16px leadership-panel body copy; white “Proof in practice.” on the green→cyan field ≈ 1.8–2.2:1; newsletter body on the cyan end ≈ 2.0:1. Direct must decide where the accents may carry text.
